02 / WALLET ANATOMY

PnL is a screenshot.
Behavior is the tape.

A wallet can look brilliant because one bag never sold, awful because old entries are missing, or active because dust got pushed into it. VIPR keeps the parts separate.

OPEN THE FULL RECEIPT ↗
01

Timing

How early the wallet enters relative to observable market birth. If market birth is unknown, timing loses confidence.

20%
02

Exit quality

Whether gains become realized exits or disappear in a roundtrip. Open-only history is not treated as complete.

18%
03

Discipline

Concentration, churn and repeat behavior across independent positions — not one lucky moonshot.

17%
04

Survival

What the wallet does when launches fail, liquidity dries up or the thesis breaks.

13%
05

Repeatability

Patterns only count when they show up across enough complete, independent samples.

17%
06

Evidence

Provenance, tape completeness and whether the source data can actually support the claim.

15%

04 / ARCHETYPES

Wallets have habits.
VIPR names the pattern.

Archetypes compress recurring observable behavior. They are descriptions, not identity claims, predictions or accusations.

01EARLY

FIRST WAVE

Repeatedly gets in early with enough complete samples for timing to mean something.

timing ↑ · repeat ↑
02FAST

SNIPER

Very early entries, selective tape and short holds. Fast does not automatically mean good.

timing ↑ · hold ↓
03HOLD

DIAMOND SCALES

Holds conviction names longer while still completing exits instead of never selling.

winners held longer
04RECOVER

SCAVENGER

Catches dislocations and exits quickly when the tape shows an opportunity.

fast recovery tape
05RISK

ROUNDTRIPPER

Frequently gives back large unrealized gains before exit. Receipt shows the give-back.

peak → exit gap
06LATE

LATE VENOM

Repeatedly enters after the crowd expands and market age is already high.

timing ↓

07 / TRENCH USE CASES

Built for the moment
before you trust a wallet.

VIPR is not an auto-trader. It is a research surface for checking whether the reputation story matches the on-chain behavior.

BEFORE FOLLOWING

“Is this wallet actually early?”

Inspect timing across complete samples instead of trusting one screenshot or one recent 20×.

timing · repeatability · evidence
AFTER A RUN

“Did they actually exit?”

Separate unrealized peaks from completed exits and see whether the wallet repeatedly roundtrips gains.

exits · discipline · survival
WHEN DATA IS THIN

“What can we honestly say?”

VIPR can show a live public-RPC tape and still refuse to manufacture a full score when provenance is incomplete.

unknown ≠ zero
