# Brand

## Name

**VIPR** — short for the viper mascot and visually native to crypto/terminal culture.

## Line

**Every wallet leaves tracks.**

## Mascot

A neon-green pixel snake: observant, patient, mischievous, never cute enough to undermine the product's evidence-first tone.

## Voice

Short, specific and receipt-driven. Prefer `verified entry at +11m` over `alpha wallet`. Prefer `evidence low` over pretending an incomplete tape is complete.

## Visual system

- background: near-black / deep green
- primary accent: toxic lime
- secondary text: muted grey-green
- bright color means evidence, status or focus — not decoration everywhere
